**Gizmo** is a complete solution for managing entertainment venues: PC and gaming clubs, internet cafes, VR spaces, lounges and more. One system covers floor service, sales and the register, customer management, loyalty, reports and control of the club's PCs.

## <icon code="rocket" /> Where to start

1. Sign up on the Gizmo website -- your account is used to activate the license and receive updates.
2. Install and set up the system step by step with the [Quick install](./instalation/quick-install.md) guide.
3. Before the first shift, show your operators the [Operator quick guide](./tutorials/operations/operator-guide.md).

> [!TIP]
>
> Moving from Gizmo V2? All the steps for moving your club settings are described in the [Migration to v3](./tutorials/migration-to-v3.md) guide.
