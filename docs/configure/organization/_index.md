Organization settings are in the **Organization** section of the Gizmo Manager settings.

Here you find the main details of your company: contact details, taxes and currency, branches and user agreements.

The section contains these subsections:

-  [General](./general.md) -- the name, contacts and regional settings of the organization.

-  [Subscription](./subscription.md) -- information about your active Gizmo license.

-  [Taxes & Currency](./taxes-and-currency.md) -- currency and taxes.

-  [Branches](./branches/_index.md) -- managing the branches of your chain.

-  [User agreements](./user-agreements/_index.md) -- agreement texts for guests.
