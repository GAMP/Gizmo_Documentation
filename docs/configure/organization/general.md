**IMAGE: the Settings -> Organization -> General page with the organization information, regional settings and default work schedule**

In this section you fill in the main information about the organization, the regional settings and the work schedule.

## Organization information

-  **Company name** -- the name of your organization. Include the legal form, for example "My Company LLC".

-  **Email** -- the email address of your organization.

-  **Website** -- the website of your organization.

> [!TIP]
> 
> If the organization name does not fit in the field, use a short form of it.

## Regional settings

-  **Language** -- the language of the Gizmo Manager interface.

-  **Time zone** -- your current time zone. All database records that contain a date and time use it, for example transactions.

-  **Country/Region** -- the country or region of your organization.

## Default work schedule

Here you set the working hours of the organization. This information is used to build reports. You can also set 24/7 operation.
