Financial settings are in the **Finances** section of the Gizmo Manager settings.

Here you set up everything related to guests' money: billing for time on a host, time products, payment methods and invoicing rules.

The section contains these subsections:

-  [Registers](./registers/_index.md) -- the registers used for financial operations.

-  [Time Products](./time-products/_index.md) -- prepaid time products sold in Gizmo Client and Gizmo Manager.

-  [Tariffs](./tariffs/_index.md) -- billing profiles for time on a host.

-  [Payment methods](./paymet-methods/_index.md) -- built-in and custom payment methods.

-  [Invoices](./invoices.md) -- automatic invoicing and payment of invoices.
