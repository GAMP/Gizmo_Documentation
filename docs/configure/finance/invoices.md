This section sets how invoices are issued and paid in Gizmo.

## Options

-  **Hold deposits for unpaid session time** -- when on, the part of the deposit that equals the cost of the unpaid time of the current session is blocked: it cannot be spent at the register or withdrawn from the deposit. This option is on by default.

-  **Automatic guest login (after a deposit / time product sale)** -- when on, a guest account is signed in on the host automatically right after a deposit top-up or a time product purchase.

-  **Auto delivery on sales from the Manager** -- when on, products and time products sold through Gizmo Manager are marked as delivered automatically, without the operator's confirmation.

## Automatic invoicing

Here you set up automatic invoicing and payment for each user group.

The table has these columns:

-  **User Group** -- the group the settings apply to.

-  **Auto Invoice** -- when on, the user is invoiced automatically when the session ends.

-  **After Minutes** -- how many minutes after the session ends the invoice is issued automatically. Until the invoice is issued, the guest's billing period stays open: if the guest signs in again (for example, after the PC restarts), billing continues and the tariff's start fee is not charged again.

-  **Auto Pay From Deposits** -- when on, the issued invoice is paid automatically from the user's deposit.
