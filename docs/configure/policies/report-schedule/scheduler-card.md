**IMAGE: Delivery rule card with the Reports, Delivery Schedule and Recipients blocks**

The delivery rule card opens when you create a new rule or edit an existing one.

**Name** -- the name of the delivery rule. This field is required.

## Reports

Here you choose the reports to include in the delivery.

The table has these columns:

-  **Name** -- the report name.

-  **Report Subtype** -- the subtype of the selected report.

To add a report, click <cmd text="+ Select Report"/>, choose the report from the list (this field is required) and click <cmd text="Save"/>.

## Report delivery schedule

-  **Time** -- the time of day when reports are sent.

-  **Type** -- how often reports are sent. Options: daily and other periods.

## Recipients

Here you set up the delivery recipients.

The table has these columns:

-  **User** -- the operator who receives the report.

-  **Method** -- the delivery method.

-  **Value** -- the recipient's address or identifier (for example, an email address).

-  **Enabled** -- the recipient status (active or disabled).

To add a recipient, click <cmd text="+ Add Recipient"/>, choose an operator from the list (this field is required) and click <cmd text="Save"/>.
