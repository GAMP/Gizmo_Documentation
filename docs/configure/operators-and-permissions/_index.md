Operator and permission settings are in the **Operators and permissions** section of the Gizmo Manager settings.

Here you manage the accounts of the staff who work in Gizmo Manager and the permission sets that define which actions they can perform.

The section contains these subsections:

-  [Operators](./operators/_index.md) -- staff accounts.

-  [Permissions](./permissions/_index.md) -- permission presets for operators.
