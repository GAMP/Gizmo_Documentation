**IMAGE: the Settings -> Operators and permissions -> Operators page with the Active and Fired tabs**

Here you manage operator accounts -- the accounts of the staff who work in Gizmo Manager.

The list of operators has two tabs:

-  **Active** -- operators who currently work in the system.

-  **Fired** -- operators who were fired and lost access to the system.

The table shows the name of each operator. To edit an operator, click their row. The [operator card](./operators-card.md) opens.

To create a new operator, click <cmd text="Create Operator"/> in the corner of the page.
