**Gizmo Client** is the client app installed on the club's gaming PCs (hosts). In the Gizmo Client shell, the guest logs in, launches games and programs, buys time products and places orders.

## <icon code="info" /> In short

| **Where it runs** | On every gaming PC in the club |
|---|---|
| **Who uses it** | Club guests |
| **What it needs** | A connection to [Gizmo Server](./gizmo-server.md) over the club network |

## <icon code="monitor" /> What the guest sees

- **The login screen** -- signing in to their account.
- **The home screen** with the **Home**, **Apps** and **Shop** tabs -- launching games and programs, buying time products and placing orders.

The screens are described in the [Gizmo Client](./../../usage/gizmo-client/_index.md) section.

## <icon code="settings" /> What the club sets up

The shell's behavior is set in Gizmo Manager, in the [Client](./../../configure/client/_index.md) section: general settings and the user session, registration, password policy, notifications, the file system and help requests. The shell's look is changed with [skins](./../../configure/client/skins/_index.md), and [security profiles](./../../usage/gizmo-manager/stations/profili-bezopasnosti/_index.md) limit the guest's access to the PC's system.

## <icon code="download" /> Installation

You download the Gizmo Client installer in Gizmo Manager: <cmd text="Settings" /> -> <cmd text="Others" /> -> <cmd text="About" />, the **Downloads** block. How to install it on the gaming PCs -- see the [Quick install](./../../instalation/quick-install.md) guide, step 4.
