**Gizmo** is a complete solution for managing entertainment venues: PC and gaming clubs, internet cafes, VR spaces, lounges and more. One system covers floor service, sales and the register, customer management, loyalty, reports and control of the club's PCs.

## <icon code="users" /> Who it is for

**Gizmo** works for:

- PC clubs and internet cafes;
- lounge spaces;
- VR clubs;
- interactive exhibitions;
- tournaments and competitions;
- computer labs in schools and universities;
- digital libraries;
- and much more.

Gizmo works for a single club as well as for a chain: you can run several clubs as branches on one server.

## <icon code="layers" /> How Gizmo is built

Gizmo consists of several apps. Each one handles its own part of the club's work:

| Component | What it does | Where it runs |
|---|---|---|
| [Gizmo Server](./overview/gizmo-server.md) | The core of the system: processes operations, stores customer data and manages the other components | The club server (Windows, Linux or Docker) |
| [Gizmo Manager](./overview/gizmo-manager.md) | The operator's and administrator's workspace: the floor, sales, club settings, reports | A browser on any device |
| [Gizmo Client](./overview/gizmo-client.md) | The guest shell: login, launching games and programs, buying time and placing orders | The club's gaming PCs |
| [Gizmo Companion](./overview/gizmo-companion/_index.md) | The bridge to POS hardware: card terminals, scanners, receipt printers, and also a link to Gizmo Client for sending commands over the local network | The PC used for sales and/or another staff PC |
