Gizmo covers the club's core tasks: from seating a guest at a PC to closing the shift and analyzing revenue. Below is what you can do in the system and where to read more about it.

## <icon code="map" /> Floor service

- **Club map** -- seat occupancy, guest time and PC status in real time. More -- [Map](./../usage/gizmo-manager/map/_index.md).
- **Queue** -- when no seats are free, the guest joins the waiting line and gets a seat as one frees up. More -- [Queues](./../usage/gizmo-manager/ochered.md).
- **Reservations** -- you can reserve seats for a guest in advance. More -- [Reservations](./../usage/gizmo-manager/reservations/_index.md).
- **Remote PC control** -- turn PCs on, restart and shut them down over the network, work with files, processes and tasks. More -- [Management](./../usage/gizmo-manager/management/_index.md) and the guide [How to turn on, restart and shut down PCs remotely](./../tutorials/hosts-operations/power-management.md).

## <icon code="credit-card" /> Sales and the register

- **Tariffs and time products** -- hourly rates and discounted time products for different zones and customer groups. More -- [Tariffs](./../configure/finance/tariffs/_index.md) and [Time Products](./../configure/finance/time-products/_index.md).
- **Register** -- sell time and products, top up deposits and accept payment by different [methods](./../configure/finance/paymet-methods/_index.md). More -- [POS](./../usage/gizmo-manager/tochka-prodazh/_index.md).
- **Shifts** -- open and close an operator's shift and reconcile the cash in the register. More -- the guide [How to manage shifts in the Manager](./../tutorials/operations/shifts.md).
- **POS hardware** -- card terminals, receipt printers, barcode scanners and other hardware connect through [Gizmo Companion](./overview/gizmo-companion/_index.md).
- **Shop and warehouse** -- products, bundles and stock tracking. More -- [Shop](./../usage/gizmo-manager/magazin/_index.md).

## <icon code="users" /> Customers and staff

- **Customer accounts** -- guest profiles, their groups and current state: which host the guest is at and how much time and money they have. More -- [Users](./../usage/gizmo-manager/users/_index.md).
- **User groups** -- their own prices, discounts and access for different customer categories.
- **Operators and permissions** -- every employee works under their own account and sees only what they are allowed to. More -- [Operators and permissions](./../configure/operators-and-permissions/_index.md).

## <icon code="gift" /> Loyalty and marketing

- **Loyalty points** -- customers earn points for purchases and spend them on products and time products. More -- the guide [Loyalty points for customers](./../tutorials/loyalty/loyalty-program.md).
- **Discounts, promotions and promo codes**, **achievements**, **challenges** and the **ladder** -- tools that bring guests back to the club. More -- [Marketing](./../usage/gizmo-manager/marketing/_index.md).

## <icon code="monitor" /> Client shell

- **Launching games and programs** -- the guest picks an app in the Gizmo Client shell, and the club manages the list of apps and how they launch. More -- [Apps](./../usage/gizmo-manager/stations/prilozheniya/_index.md).
- **Security** -- security profiles limit the guest's access to the PC's system. More -- [Security Profiles](./../usage/gizmo-manager/stations/profili-bezopasnosti/_index.md).
- **Look** -- shell skins that match the club's style. More -- [Skins](./../configure/client/skins/_index.md).
- **Game saves** -- the guest's progress is kept between sessions. More -- the guide [How to keep guests' game progress between sessions](./../tutorials/client-shell/personal-files.md).

## <icon code="bar-chart-3" /> Reports and control

- **Reports** -- a summary of revenue and occupancy, logs of operations for orders, shifts, products and transactions; reports can be emailed on a schedule. More -- [Reports](./../usage/gizmo-manager/otchety/_index.md) and the guide [Reports](./../tutorials/operations/reports-guide.md).
- **Logs** -- logs of how the Gizmo system works. More -- [Logs](./../usage/gizmo-manager/logs.md).

## <icon code="server" /> Server and club chains

- **Several clubs** -- branches of a chain on one server. More -- [Branches](./../configure/organization/branches/_index.md) and the guide [Multiple clubs on one server](./../tutorials/operations/multiple-clubs-setup.md).
- **Backup** -- automatic database copies on a schedule. More -- [Backup](./../configure/server/backup.md).
- **Plugins and integrations** -- add-ons from third-party developers and connections to external services. More -- [Plugins](./../plugins/_index.md) and [For Developers](./../for-developers/_index.md).
