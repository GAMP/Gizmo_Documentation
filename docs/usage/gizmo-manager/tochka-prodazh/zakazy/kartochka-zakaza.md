The card opens when you double-click a row in the [order log](./_index.md). It holds the order contents, the payment breakdown and the user information.

## Header

The top part shows the order number, the date it was placed and two status labels -- payment and fulfillment. Next to them is the <cmd text="Actions"/> button.

The number of delivered items is shown separately, along with the **All Delivered** checkbox, which closes the delivery of the whole order.

## Delivered items

The list of order items. Each shows its name, unit price, quantity, amount and payment status.

## Payment breakdown

-  **Subtotal** -- the cost of the items without taxes and discounts.

-  **Taxes** -- the tax charged.

-  **Fee** -- the payment method fee.

-  **Discount** -- the discount applied.

-  **Minimum Payment** -- the amount required to process the order.

-  **Total** -- the final amount due.

-  **Paid** -- the amount paid.

The difference between **Total** and **Paid** is shown in the log in the **Left to Pay** column.

## User information

-  **User** -- who placed the order.

-  **Branch** -- the branch where the order was placed.

-  **Host** -- the gaming station the order came from.

-  **Preferred Payment Method** -- the method the user chose.

-  **Notes** -- the user's comment on the order. If there is no comment, "No notes from the customer" is shown.
