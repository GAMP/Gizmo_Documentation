The tab holds the club's order log. Unlike the **Orders** section in the left menu, which shows orders in progress, this tab keeps the full history, including closed and voided orders.

## Order list

-  **Order Number** -- the order's sequence number.

-  **Date** -- the date and time the order was placed.

-  **User** -- the user who placed the order.

-  **Branch** -- the branch where the order was placed.

-  **Host** -- the gaming station the order came from.

-  **Operator** -- the staff member who processed the order.

-  **Shift Number** -- the shift the order belongs to.

-  **Register** -- the register where the payment was made.

-  **Payment Status** -- the payment state.

-  **Delivery Status** -- the delivery state.

-  **Voided** -- marks a voided order.

-  **Left to Pay** -- the unpaid part of the amount.

-  **Total** -- the full order amount.

Double-click a row to open the [Order card](./kartochka-zakaza.md).

## Related sections

Orders are paid on the [Sales](./../prodazhi.md) tab. Summary data on orders for a period is available in the [Reports](./../../otchety/_index.md) section, on the **Order Log** and **Order Statistics** tabs.
