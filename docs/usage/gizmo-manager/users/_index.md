This page brings together the club's customer accounts and the groups they are sorted into. The main list shows the state of each guest: which group they are in, which host they are at, and how much money, time and points they have. The second tab handles the rules shared by a whole category of customers.

## Sections

- [Users](./users/_index.md) -- the list of all the club's accounts, actions on a guest and tools for keeping the database clean.
- [User card](./users/user-card.md) -- everything about one guest: details, money, time, points, purchase and session history.
- [User Groups](./user-groups/_index.md) -- the list of groups the accounts are sorted into.
- [User group card](./user-groups/user-group-card.md) -- the group rules: how time is paid for, which hosts are accessible, which discount applies.

## What the section is for

From here the club manages its customer database. On this page you can:

- create new accounts and edit existing ones;
- log guests in on hosts and log them out;
- top up deposits, make sales and give refunds;
- ban and unban users, delete and restore accounts;
- sort customers into groups and set the rules for each group;
- clean up the database -- find duplicates and import customers as a list from a file.

> [!NOTE]
>
> The group a guest is in defines their discount, the hosts available to them and how they can pay for time. The group links the customer to a [discount group](./../marketing/_index.md) and to [host groups](./../stations/station-groups/_index.md).
