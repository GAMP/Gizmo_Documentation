The section is for managing the product range and stock: here you create products and their groups, track stock and record warehouse operations. The section has eight tabs, and **Warehouses** opens first.

The section opens from <cmd text="Shop"/> in the left menu.

## Tabs

Product range:

-  [**Warehouses**](./warehouses.md) -- the places where products are stored. Each warehouse shows its name, type, branch and status. Created with the <cmd text="Create Warehouse"/> button.

-  **Products** -- the list of items available for sale. Each shows its name, barcode, status, reward, cost, price, type and group.

-  **Bundles** -- items that combine several products and are sold as one.

-  **Product Groups** -- the sections that sort products on the cashier's screen. You can change the order of groups and of products inside a group: open a product group to sort it.

Stock tracking:

-  **Stock Movement** -- the history of stock operations: date, item name, transaction type, starting and ending quantity, warehouse and operator.

-  **Stock Count** -- stock counts. Each shows the date, warehouse, branch, operator, type, number of records, shortage and surplus.

-  **Warehouse Operations** -- operations with stock: date, branch, warehouse, type, operator, status and quantity.

-  **Product Stock** -- the current quantity of each item with its group, type, barcode, branch and warehouse.

## How it fits together

A product is created on the **Products** tab and belongs to one of the **product groups**; the group defines which tab the item appears on in the [Sales](./../tochka-prodazh/prodazhi.md) screen. The product quantity is tracked in a **warehouse**: a sale deducts the item, an incoming delivery adds to the stock, and a **stock count** brings the recorded data in line with the actual quantities.
