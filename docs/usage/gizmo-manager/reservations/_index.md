This page brings together the hosts booked in advance. The calendar shows host occupancy over time -- which hosts are already reserved and for which hours -- and the log keeps the reservation records themselves, with their status and payment status.

## Sections

-  [Calendar](./calendar.md) -- the grid of host occupancy by date and half-hour slots.

-  [Reservation card](./reservation-card.md) -- the conditions of one reservation: time, duration, owner, hosts.

-  [Reservation log](./reservation-log.md) -- the list of all the club's reservations, upcoming and past.

## What the section is for

From here the club works with hosts booked in advance. On this page you can:

-  make a reservation for a specific host and time, with or without payment;

-  check room occupancy for a given date;

-  find a specific guest's reservation;

-  review the history: whether a reservation took place and whether it was paid.

> [!NOTE]
> 
> The reservation rules -- how far ahead and for how long reservations are allowed -- are set separately in the settings, in [Reservations](./../../../configure/policies/reservations.md).
