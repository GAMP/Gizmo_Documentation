title: Navigation panel
order: 4
---

-  **Map** -- an interactive layout of the club where the administrator sees the status of the gaming stations, makes sales and watches guests being logged in.

-  **Hosts** -- the section for setting up gaming stations, assigning them to zones and branches, and configuring the club map, the client shell and tasks on the hosts.

-  **POS** -- here you sell time products and products, and view the logs of sales, invoices, balance top-ups and other financial operations.

-  **Users** -- managing customer accounts and their groups.

-  **Reservations** -- here you can reserve any gaming station for a guest in advance, if this is allowed in the settings.

-  **Monitoring** -- lets you view several client PC screens at once and spot unwanted actions by guests.

-  **Logs** -- collects the app's error logs. They are useful when you contact support.

-  **Reports** -- all the club's analytics in one place, with flexible filters and sections.

-  **Queue** -- automatically lines guests up for gaming stations when the club is full and more people want to play.

-  **Marketing** -- news and banners in the client shell, discounts, promo codes and a loyalty program for your customers.

-  **Shop** -- here you create and set up the product range and manage the warehouse, write-offs and stock counts.

-  **Management** -- remote access to the file system, the terminal and the tasks on client PCs. You can open files on a guest's PC without the guest noticing, run scripts and end processes -- for example, close a frozen game.

-  **Settings** -- the largest section. It holds parameters that are usually changed rarely, so set them up carefully right away to avoid coming back to them later.

-  **Orders** -- the list of orders from the client shell. From here the administrator manages orders: confirms, charges and delivers them.
