**IMAGE: Search bar at the top of Gizmo Manager with results grouped by users, hosts, products and time products**

The search tool is at the top of the page. You can search in the following categories:

-  Users

-  Hosts

-  Products

-  Time products

## Available buttons

### For hosts

-  **Log In** -- logs the selected user account in on this device.

-  **Enable Admin Mode** -- turns on admin mode (full access) on this device.

### For users

-  **Log In** -- logs the selected user account in on the selected device.

-  **Close Balance** -- logs the customer out of the gaming station and lets you close the session and/or the active invoice.

-  **Deposit** -- tops up the selected customer's deposit.

-  **Sales** -- opens the sales section with the selected user.

### For products and time products

Clicking a product or a time product opens its settings card.
