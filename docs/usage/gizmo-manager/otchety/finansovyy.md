The report summarizes the money turnover for a period. It opens from the [Reports](./_index.md) section, **All Reports** tab -> **Financial Report**.

## Filters

They are set at the top of the screen and applied with the <cmd text="Apply Filters"/> button:

-  **Operator** -- limits the report to the operations of one staff member.

-  **Register** -- the register where the operations were made.

-  **Report Type** -- the level of detail. The default is **Simple**.

-  **Period** -- a preset interval or a custom date and time range.

## Report blocks

### Deposits and Withdrawals

Top-ups and withdrawals from user accounts. Shows the number of operations and the amount; the total is shown in the **Deposits and Withdrawals Total** row.

### Sales

Sales for the period. Columns: **Quantity**, **Tax**, **Total Cost**, **Amount**. The total is the **Total Sales** row.

### Payments for Past Sales

Amounts received for invoices issued before the start of the selected period.

### Voids

Voided sales. The columns are the same as in the **Sales** block. The total is the **Voided Items Total** row.

### Pay Ins and Outs

Cash movements not related to sales:

-  **Pay Ins** -- the number of operations and the amount of pay ins.

-  **Pay Outs** -- the number of operations and the amount of pay outs.

-  **Pay Ins and Outs Total** -- the difference.

### Balances

Totals by the payment methods set up in the club. Below them are **Total Tax**, **Total Cost** and **Grand Total**.
