Gizmo Manager is the app where most of the work with Gizmo happens. It is the club operator's main workplace.

The app opens in a browser at the server address. The interface is the same on every page: the navigation panel with the sections is on the left, the bar with search, notifications and the operator menu is at the top, and the content of the selected section is in the center.

> [!IMPORTANT]
>
> You can find the Gizmo Manager connection settings in [Network](./../../configure/server/network.md).

## What you can do in the app

An operator's work in Manager comes down to a few everyday tasks:

- **Run the floor** -- log guests in, watch host occupancy and time on the [Map](./map/_index.md), add guests to the waiting line in [Queues](./ochered.md) when no hosts are left, and make [reservations](./../../configure/policies/reservations.md) for customers.
- **Sell products and services** -- sell time and products at the [POS](./tochka-prodazh/_index.md), take payments and manage deposits.
- **Configure the club** -- set up computers and endpoints in [Hosts](./stations/_index.md), manage the product range in the [Shop](./magazin/_index.md), and run promotions and discounts in [Marketing](./marketing/_index.md).
- **Build and collect reports** -- analyze data in [Reports](./otchety/_index.md), track errors in [Logs](./logs.md), and keep stock records in [Warehouses](./magazin/warehouses.md).

## What the app consists of

All Manager pages follow the same layout, so the common elements are described separately, in [Interface](./interface/_index.md):

- [Navigation panel](./interface/navigation-panel.md) -- on the left, the main way to move between the sections of the app.
- [Search](./interface/search.md) -- at the top center. Finds the information you need: users, hosts, products and time products.
- [Notifications](./interface/notifications.md) -- at the top right, report orders, help requests and events.
- [Operator menu](./interface/operator-menu.md) -- in the top right corner: change password, theme and sign out.

> [!NOTE]
>
> Signing in to Gizmo Manager and operator authorization are described in [Login screen](./login-screen.md).
