The section shows screenshots of client PCs, so the operator can see what is happening on several gaming stations at once and spot unwanted actions by guests.

The section opens from <cmd text="Monitoring"/> in the left menu. Access to it is set by the permissions of the **Monitoring** category in the operator's permission set -- see [Permission card](./../../configure/operators-and-permissions/permissions/permission-card.md).

## What you can configure

-  **Which PCs to watch** -- automatic screenshot refresh is turned on for the selected PCs. You can also refresh the screenshots of all PCs manually, all at once.

-  **Size** of the screenshot windows.

-  **Quality** of the screenshots -- the higher it is, the sharper the image, but the higher the network load.

-  **Refresh rate** of the screenshots.

-  **Display** -- you can hide PCs that are not connected right now, move the watched PCs to the top of the list and hide the numbers on the screenshot windows.

If you need not only to look at a PC's screen but also to do something on it, connect to it with remote access -- see [Remote access](./map/remote-access.md).
