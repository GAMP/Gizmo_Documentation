---
title: Feeds
order: 0.5
---

**Feeds** are RSS feeds whose entries are shown to guests on the Gizmo Client home page together with the club's news. For example, you can add a gaming news or esports news feed.

For each feed you set:

-  **Name** -- the feed title the guest sees;

-  **URL** -- the address of the RSS feed;

-  **Max Entries** -- how many of the latest feed entries to show.
