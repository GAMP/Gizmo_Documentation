title: Discount group card
order: 1
---

The card holds the name of the discount group and the discounts linked to it. The card opens with the <cmd text="Create Discount Group"/> button on the **Discount Groups** tab.

-  **Name** -- the discount group name. Required.

-  **Discounts** -- the block with the discounts linked to the group.

## Linking a discount to a discount group

To link a discount to a discount group, select the discount in the drop-down list in the **Discounts** block and save the changes on the page.

## Unlinking a discount from a discount group

To unlink a discount from a discount group, click the cross icon next to the discount in the **Discounts** block and save the changes on the page.

> [!TIP]
>
> Remember to link the new discount group to a [user group](./../../users/user-groups/user-group-card.md).
